# Home Assistant Add-on: Tolle Bible Study

![Supports aarch64][aarch64-shield] ![Supports amd64][amd64-shield]

AI-assisted Bible study in your Home Assistant sidebar: upload and compare study notes with trusted sources, research passages at any depth, get insights, build group studies, reflect prayerfully on your calling and track your reading. Works with Ollama, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or on-device AI. Everything is encrypted.

See the **Documentation** tab for setup.
