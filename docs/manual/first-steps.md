# First steps

If you used the desktop setup assistant, steps 1 and 2 are already done. Skip to step 3.

## 1. Create your account

The first time you open Tolle you're asked to create an account. The first account is the **administrator**: it can add other people and change server-wide settings. Everything you store is encrypted and only visible to you.

There's no "forgot password" email, so keep your password somewhere safe. An administrator can reset passwords ([how](security-and-backups.md#forgotten-password)).

## 2. Choose an AI

Open **Settings → AI**:

1. Choose a **provider**:
   - **Ollama**: free and private, running on your computer or another one on your network.
   - **OpenAI (ChatGPT)**, **Google Gemini** or **Anthropic Claude**: paste an API key from the provider.
   - **On-device**: runs a small model inside the app on this device.
2. Click **Save and refresh**, then pick a **default model**.
3. Turn **Thinking** on if your model supports it and you want deeper (slower) answers.

[More about choosing an AI](ai.md).

## 3. Check the study sites

**Settings → Sources** lists the sites Tolle reads for each passage (StudyLight, BibleStudyTools, Enduring Word, BibleProject and the Bible text). Press **Test** next to each with a reference such as `John 3:16`. A ✓ means it works. If a site has changed its layout, edit its address there ([how](study-sites.md)).

## 4. Turn on notifications

**Settings → Devices & notifications**:

1. Click **Allow notifications on this device** on each computer, phone or tablet you use.
2. Use the **Notify** switch on each device to choose which ones get reminders.
3. On the **Reading** tab, turn on the **reading reminder** and set a time and a daily goal.

## 5. Connect your phone (optional)

Install the phone app and scan the code in **Settings → Devices & notifications → Connect a phone or tablet**. See [Phones & tablets](phones-and-tablets.md).

## 6. Add other people (servers only)

As the administrator: **Settings → Account → Add user**. Give each person their username and password; they can change the password themselves under **Settings → Account**.

## 7. Make it yours (administrators)

- **Settings → Features**: hide the parts of Tolle your group doesn't use.
- **Settings → AI instructions**: adjust the AI's wording to suit your tradition or group.
- **Settings → Bible & study**: Bible translation, your tradition (so the AI uses familiar language), default study depth and theme.
